About Socratic Scribe

Socratic Scribe is a clean, distraction-free study assistant built to help students analyze, digest, and refine their writing through guided feedback.

Why I Built This

Studying shouldn't feel cluttered or overwhelming. Designed with a tablet-first approach, Socratic Scribe gives students a calm, focused digital workspace—combining paper-like aesthetics with powerful analysis tools.

How It Works

  • Input: Paste or type your paragraph into the editor.
  • Choose Your Speed: Switch between Fast for quick checks or Deep for detailed breakdowns.
  • Analyze: Get instant, structured feedback on your writing without losing your place.
